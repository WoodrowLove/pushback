## Anti-patterns to avoid

- ❌ "Hey, I built [hype word] AI [hype word] for medical bills, would love your support 🚀"
- ❌ Asking for retweets on first contact
- ❌ Sending to >5 people the same hour (looks like a script)
- ❌ Multiple follow-ups before a 7-day pause
- ❌ Mentioning revenue / pricing in first DM
- ❌ Sending links without context

## What "good" looks like

- Specific to the recipient's work
- Names what they've done before asking
- Free, no strings, "tell me what's broken"
- Short — they read in <30 seconds
- A real public touch (reply, follow, like) before/with the DM so the recipient sees you're a real account
